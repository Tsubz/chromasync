Chromasync is a calm, single-purpose breathing companion. Living light and generative sound expand and settle with each breath, so you can follow the rhythm with your eyes open, or closed with gentle vibrations.

BREATHING PATTERNS
• Resonance breathing: adjustable from 4.5 to 7 breaths per minute (5.5 by default), the range where heart-rate variability peaks for most adults.
• Physiological sigh: a double inhale and a long exhale.
• Box breathing (4-4-4-4).
• 4-7-8 Relax and Deep Unwind (4-2-6), with an option to shorten breath holds.
• Free Breath: no tempo, hold the screen to inhale and release to exhale.

FIVE ATMOSPHERES
Aurora, Ocean, Forest Rain, Warm Hearth and Astral Void. Each pairs visuals, ambient sound and a matching rhythm. Warm Hearth uses an amber palette with very little blue light for evenings.

DESIGNED TO GET OUT OF THE WAY
• Session timer (3, 5 or 10 minutes, or unlimited) that ends on a full breath.
• Pure-black "eyes closed" mode with vibration cues.
• Optional 432 / 440 / 528 Hz tunings and headphone binaural beats.
• English and French.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your settings stay on your device. Works offline. Free and open source.

THE SCIENCE, HONESTLY
Each pattern links to the research behind it, with its limits: for example, studies on 432 Hz music are small pilots, and evidence that binaural beats change brainwaves is mixed.

Chromasync is a relaxation tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. Stop if you feel dizzy, and ask a doctor before breath-holding exercises if you are pregnant or have a heart or respiratory condition.
